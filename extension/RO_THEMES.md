## Usage

### Option 1: Manual Search
1. Click the extension icon
2. Type your research topic (e.g., "ruimtelijke ordening", "smart cities")
3. Click "🔍 Search Papers"

### Option 2: Auto-Analysis
1. Navigate to a webpage about urban planning, smart cities, etc.
2. Click "📖 Analyze This Page"
3. The extension will detect relevant topics and show papers automatically

## Quality Scoring

Papers are evaluated on 6 metrics:
- **Citation Impact** (25%): Research influence
- **Publication Venue** (20%): Journal/conference prestige
- **Methodology** (18%): Research rigor
- **Data Transparency** (15%): Code/data availability
- **Novel Contribution** (12%): Innovation level
- **Author Expertise** (10%): Team credentials

## Settings

Adjust:
- **Min Quality Score**: Filter papers by minimum score (default: 75)
- **Max Results**: Limit results displayed (default: 10)
